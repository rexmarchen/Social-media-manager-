# My Engineering & Career Notes

## Background & Focus
I'm a Computer Science & Engineering student focused on backend systems, distributed architectures, and applied AI. I've built projects in distributed systems, real-time collaboration tools, and machine learning pipelines.

## Hands-on Learnings
- **Distributed Caching & Latency**: Shipped a custom caching layer that reduced API latency by 40% for a systems project. A key takeaway was understanding the balance between premature optimization and under-optimization—measuring p95 and p99 metrics before and after caching made all the difference.
- **Hackathon Experience**: Placed in the top 10 at a regional hackathon building a real-time collaboration workspace using WebSockets and conflict-resolution mechanisms.

## Perspectives & Opinions
- I believe many engineering students over-index on modern UI frameworks and under-index on reading production code, understanding networking, and building solid backend fundamentals.
- The best way to understand distributed systems is to intentionally break them: simulate network partitions, edge cases, and crash recovery.
